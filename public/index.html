A trip planner for Havasupai hikers

Walk into the canyon ready, walk out grinning.

Havasupai is ten miles each way with a full pack, desert heat, and no room for guessing. Havasu Prep builds you a training plan matched to your fitness, tracks every session, keeps your packing list honest, and puts permits, rules, and emergency numbers one tap away.

Free to use. Your training data belongs to you.

A plan that meets you where you are

Answer six quick questions about your activity level and trip date. Get a week by week training plan with walk, hike, strength, and long hike days that ramp up and taper right.

Training you can actually track

Check off each session, log miles and minutes, watch your streak and weekly completion climb. Every workout is one tap, built for tired thumbs after a long hike.

A packing list that starts smart

Begin with a field tested base list covering permits, water, camp, food, clothing, and safety. Check items off, add your own, remove what you do not need.

Trail intel and emergency numbers

Distances, permit check in, campground rules, flash flood safety, and the numbers that matter: tribal police, the tourist office, the inn, and the nearest hospitals.

Notes for the details

Flight times, rental car confirmations, the fry bread stand hours, that thing your friend said about Mooney Falls. Pin the important ones to the top.

A countdown that keeps you honest

Set your trip date and the home screen counts the days. Your current training week lines up automatically, so you always know what is on deck.

No app store, no account maze. It runs in your browser on any device, and your data is tied to your Google sign in.
